# Sinonimia

**Plain-language dictionary of difficult words — paperwork, justice, health, finance, daily life — every entry written to be understood on the first read.**

---

## What is Sinonimia?

Sinonimia is a digital tool for looking up the words that show up on official forms, court notices, medical leaflets and everyday bureaucracy. Each entry explains the word in short, everyday language, with an example sentence and a pictogram.

It is **not** a game, a quiz, or a clinical assessment — it is a dictionary with two light practice games on top. The complete topic list and the games live in [`activities.md`](activities.md).

---

## Key features

### ✅ Designed for autonomy

- **Easy Reading**: short sentences, everyday vocabulary, one idea per entry
- **Example first**: every word is shown inside a short example sentence so the meaning lands in context
- **No pressure**: no timers, no negative scores; the two games reward progress with encouragement, never with stars or failure messages

### ✅ Accessible to everyone

- **Adjustable font size** from the menu
- **High contrast** and visible focus rings
- **Keyboard navigation** and `aria-live` on dynamic messages
- **Pictograms** from [ARASAAC](https://arasaac.org) on every entry, shared across languages when they represent the same concept

### ✅ Privacy by default

No backend, no database, no telemetry. Words you look up and any sentences you write stay in `localStorage` on your own device. You can clear them at any time from the **Settings** route.

### ✅ In two languages (more are easy to add)

- 🇪🇸 **Español** (default)
- 🇬🇧 **English**

## Getting started

### 1. Open the app

Visit **[sinonimia.apptonomia.uk](https://sinonimia.apptonomia.uk)** or open `index.html` from a local server. The full step-by-step with **four ways to open Sinonimia** (internet, ZIP, Python, Node.js) lives in [`quick-guide.md`](quick-guide.md).

### 2. Search a word

Type a word in the search box at the top, or pick a topic from the menu to browse by category. You can also browse by letter with the alphabet strip.

### 3. Read the entry

Each entry shows the **word**, a short **definition** in everyday language, an **example sentence**, and a **pictogram**. The synonym is repeated inside the example sentence so you can spot it.

### 4. Try the games (optional)

When you want a quick practice session, the **Match** game asks you to recognise a word from its definition, and the **Order** game asks you to rebuild a scrambled example sentence. Sessions are short (5–10 questions) and never punitive.

### 5. Change language

Tap the language button (🇪🇸 or 🇬🇧) at the top of the screen.

## Example of use

Imagine you receive a letter that says **"acude a la cita con tu médico de cabecera"**. You don't know what "médico de cabecera" means. Open Sinonimia, search for "médico de cabecera", and the entry shows:

> **Definición**: Es el médico que te atiende siempre que vas al centro de salud. Es tu médico de referencia.
>
> **Ejemplo**: *Cuando me duele la garganta, voy a mi **médico de cabecera** y él me dice lo que tengo que hacer.*

You now know who to ask for, and the example sentence shows the term in the same shape it appears on the letter.

## Topics currently shipped

| Topic | What it covers |

| **Administración** | Public administration, paperwork, certificates. |

| **Hacienda y finanzas** | Taxes, bank, social security, payroll. |
| **Vivienda y vida cotidiana** | Home, neighbourhood, daily life. |
| **Educación y trabajo** | School, training, employment, contracts. |
